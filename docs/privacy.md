# Privacy Policy: Jade Promise Mahjong

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Developer: Michael Schwarz · Contact: [support@eurobaselabs.com](mailto:support@eurobaselabs.com)

## The short version
- We do **not** run servers that collect your data. Your progress, settings and game certificates stay on your device.
- The game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy and ad choices**.
- There are no in-app purchases at the moment. If they are added, Google Play or the App Store handles payment, and we never see your payment details.

## What stays on your device
- Game progress, stars, statistics, settings and your hint balance
- The game you are currently playing (its seed and move list)
- Dead-end certificates (the seed, board layout and moves of a game). These leave your device only if you choose **Share**.

We have no access to this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game shows a small banner ad at the top of the screen while you play, full-screen ads **between boards only** (never during a board), and optional rewarded videos when you ask for extra hints. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:
- device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
- IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information;
- ad interactions (views, clicks).

**Consent.** In regions where consent is required, a Google-certified consent form appears before any ads load. If you decline, only limited, non-personalised ads may be shown. On iOS, the system "Allow tracking" prompt decides whether the IDFA can be used. You can reopen your choices in Settings.

**How Google uses data:** <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

In-app purchases are not offered at the moment.

## Purchases
If in-app purchases are offered in the future, they are processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase happened, so we can unlock it on your device. We do not receive or store payment information.

## Children
The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Ads are requested with a general-audience content rating.

## Your rights
Since we hold no personal data on servers, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls (<https://myadcenter.google.com>) and your device's ad settings (reset or delete the advertising ID). EEA/UK residents can withdraw consent at any time in **Settings → Privacy and ad choices**.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

## Contact
Questions about this policy or your privacy: [support@eurobaselabs.com](mailto:support@eurobaselabs.com).
